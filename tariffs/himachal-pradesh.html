What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Himachal Pradesh

A 3 kWp on-grid system in Himachal Pradesh generates roughly 4,272 units per year at local irradiance. At ₹4.1/kWh, if you use most of it yourself, that's around ₹17,515 saved annually. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, payback lands near 6.7 years — before counting any export income.

DISCOM & net metering in Himachal Pradesh

Your distribution company is HPSEBL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹2.5/kWh — lower than the ₹4.1 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Himachal Pradesh?

At ₹4.1/kWh, Himachal Pradesh's tariff is on the lower side, so payback is slower than high-tariff states. Solar still works, but maximising self-consumption matters more here.
