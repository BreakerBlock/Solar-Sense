What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Himachal Pradesh

A 3 kW on-grid system suits a Himachal Pradesh home using about 450 units a month. At 5 peak sun hours it generates roughly 4,271 units a year. The figures below are the solar calculator's own output for Himachal Pradesh: it prices the system at its benchmark rate including the net meter, assumes you use 65% of the units yourself at ₹4.1/kWh and export the rest at the ₹2.5/kWh buyback rate. That works out to about ₹15,120 a year and a payback of about 8.7 years after the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy. If your home uses nearly all of the solar units itself, the benefit rises towards ₹17,511 a year and payback falls to about 7.5 years, and a quote below the benchmark shortens it further.

DISCOM & net metering in Himachal Pradesh

Your distribution company is HPSEBL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹2.5/kWh — lower than the ₹4.1 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Himachal Pradesh?

At ₹4.1/kWh, Himachal Pradesh's tariff is on the lower side, so payback is slower than high-tariff states. Solar still works, but maximising self-consumption matters more here.
