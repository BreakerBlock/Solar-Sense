What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Maharashtra

A 3 kW on-grid system suits a Maharashtra home using about 495 units a month. At 5.5 peak sun hours it generates roughly 4,709 units a year. The figures below are the solar calculator's own output for Maharashtra: it prices the system at its benchmark rate including the net meter, assumes you use 65% of the units yourself at ₹6.5/kWh and export the rest at the ₹3.06/kWh buyback rate. That works out to about ₹24,940 a year and a payback of about 5.3 years after the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy. If your home uses nearly all of the solar units itself, the benefit rises towards ₹30,608 a year and payback falls to about 4.3 years, and a quote below the benchmark shortens it further.

DISCOM & net metering in Maharashtra

Your distribution company is MSEDCL / Adani / Tata. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹3.06/kWh — lower than the ₹6.5 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Maharashtra?

With one of India's higher tariffs at ₹6.5/kWh, Maharashtra offers some of the fastest solar payback in the country. Every unit you generate offsets an expensive grid unit. Combined with 5.5 peak sun hours a day, the numbers are strongly in your favour. Use the solar panel cost calculator for Maharashtra to size a system against your own bill.
