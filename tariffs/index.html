FAQ

Solar by state: frequently asked questions

Which state has the fastest rooftop solar payback?
In the solar calculator, Rajasthan: a 3 kW on-grid system there makes about 5,548 units a year at 6.5 peak sun hours and pays back in about 4.5 years. Delhi (4.8 years), Haryana (5.2), Maharashtra (5.3) and Punjab (5.4) follow. The slowest is Goa at about 9.3 years, because its ₹3.9 tariff is the lowest of the 22 states, so each solar unit saves less.
Does the electricity tariff or the buyback rate matter more for payback?
The tariff, for most homes. The calculator assumes about 65% of solar units are used at home, each saving the full tariff, and only 35% exported at the buyback rate. Haryana has the lowest buyback rate of the 22 states (₹2) yet the third-fastest payback, because its tariff is ₹7.2. West Bengal gets less sun than Bihar (4.5 against 5 peak sun hours) but pays back sooner (5.9 against 6.1 years) because its tariff is ₹7.5 against ₹6.1.
Is the solar subsidy the same in every state?
The central PM Surya Ghar subsidy is the same in most states: ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third, up to ₹78,000. Special category states and UTs, including Assam and the other North-Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, get 10% more, up to ₹85,800. Some states also add their own top-up, such as Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra and Rajasthan (see state top-ups). The calculator includes only the general central subsidy.
How up to date are these tariff and buyback rates?
They are indicative rates from published DISCOM tariffs as of January 2026, and the calculator uses the same figures. State commissions revise tariffs every year, and a few have since set new rooftop solar rates, such as Maharashtra's ₹2.82 per unit for FY2026-27 and Karnataka's 2026 rates for new systems, which the net metering guide covers. Check your latest bill or your DISCOM's current tariff order before deciding.
How do I use the solar calculator for my state?
Open your state's page from the list above and use its calculator button, or choose your state in the calculator's first step. The calculator then applies that state's tariff, buyback rate and peak sun hours to your bill or appliance list and shows the system size, cost after subsidy, yearly saving and payback.
