What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Delhi

A 3 kW on-grid system suits a Delhi home using about 495 units a month. At 5.5 peak sun hours it generates roughly 4,709 units a year. The figures below are the solar calculator's own output for Delhi: it prices the system at its benchmark rate including the net meter, assumes you use 65% of the units yourself at ₹7/kWh and export the rest at the ₹3.5/kWh buyback rate. That works out to about ₹27,195 a year and a payback of about 4.8 years after the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy. If your home uses nearly all of the solar units itself, the benefit rises towards ₹32,963 a year and payback falls to about 4.0 years, and a quote below the benchmark shortens it further.

DISCOM & net metering in Delhi

Your distribution company is BSES / Tata Power Delhi. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹3.5/kWh — lower than the ₹7 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Delhi?

With one of India's higher tariffs at ₹7/kWh, Delhi offers some of the fastest solar payback in the country. Every unit you generate offsets an expensive grid unit. Combined with 5.5 peak sun hours a day, the numbers are strongly in your favour. Use the rooftop solar calculator for Delhi to size a system against your own bill.

Solar in Delhi: frequently asked questions

How much does a 3 kW rooftop solar system cost in Delhi?
The solar calculator prices a 3 kW on-grid system at ₹2,09,552 including the net meter; market quotes are typically ₹1.6–2.4 lakh before subsidy (see the solar panel cost guide). After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy the calculator’s net cost is ₹1,31,552. In Delhi that system makes about 4,709 units a year, worth about ₹27,195 a year at the ₹7/kWh tariff and ₹3.5 buyback, so it pays back in about 4.8 years.
How much solar subsidy can I get in Delhi?
Households in Delhi get the central PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy of ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW: ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and a maximum of ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more, worked out pro rata in between (2.5 kW gets ₹69,000). Since September 2026 Delhi also gives its own subsidy of up to ₹26,000 per kW, capped at ₹78,000, so a 3 kW system can get up to ₹1,56,000 in total (never more than the system costs), and homes averaging up to 400 units a month can get up to 3 kW free through the government's utility-led route. The calculator includes only the central subsidy, so your net cost can be lower than shown here; check your eligibility with BSES / Tata Power Delhi or the Delhi solar portal. See the solar subsidy calculator for more sizes and state top-ups.
What size solar system does a home in Delhi need?
The calculator sizes about 1 kW for every 165 units of monthly use in Delhi. A typical 2 BHK using 250 units a month needs about 1.6 kW (₹67,494 after subsidy, payback about 4.6 years) and a typical 3 BHK using 450 units needs about 2.8 kW (₹1,21,715, about 4.8 years). Use the average of 12 months of bills rather than a summer month; the 2 BHK and 3 BHK solar guide has more home sizes.
How many units does 1 kW of solar generate in Delhi?
At 5.5 peak sun hours, 1 kW of panels in Delhi makes about 4.3 units a day, about 131 a month averaged over the year and about 1,570 a year, after the calculator’s 78% allowance for real-world losses such as heat, dust and wiring. Output is higher in summer and lower in the monsoon, so monthly bills vary; see solar generation per kW by state.
What is the solar buyback rate in Delhi?
Under net metering with BSES / Tata Power Delhi, the calculator values exported units at ₹3.5/kWh (published rates as of January 2026), against the ₹7/kWh you pay for grid power. Each exported unit is worth about 50% of a unit you use yourself, which is why sizing solar to your own use pays back faster than oversizing for export. Buyback rates change with tariff orders, so confirm the current rate with your DISCOM; net metering explained covers how credits work and compares buyback rates across 22 states.
