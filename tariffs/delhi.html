What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Delhi

A 3 kWp on-grid system in Delhi generates roughly 4,698 units per year at local irradiance. At ₹7/kWh, if you use most of it yourself, that's around ₹32,886 saved annually. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, payback lands near 3.6 years — before counting any export income.

DISCOM & net metering in Delhi

Your distribution company is BSES / Tata Power Delhi. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹3.5/kWh — lower than the ₹7 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Delhi?

With one of India's higher tariffs at ₹7/kWh, Delhi offers some of the fastest solar payback in the country. Every unit you generate offsets an expensive grid unit. Combined with 5.5 peak sun hours a day, the numbers are strongly in your favour.
