What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Chhattisgarh

A 3 kWp on-grid system in Chhattisgarh generates roughly 4,272 units per year at local irradiance. At ₹4.8/kWh, if you use most of it yourself, that's around ₹20,506 saved annually. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, payback lands near 5.7 years — before counting any export income.

DISCOM & net metering in Chhattisgarh

Your distribution company is CSPDCL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹2.6/kWh — lower than the ₹4.8 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Chhattisgarh?

At ₹4.8/kWh, Chhattisgarh's tariff is on the lower side, so payback is slower than high-tariff states. Solar still works, but maximising self-consumption matters more here. Combined with 5 peak sun hours a day, the numbers work well.
