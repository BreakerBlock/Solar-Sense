What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Bihar

A 3 kWp on-grid system in Bihar generates roughly 4,272 units per year at local irradiance. At ₹6.1/kWh, if you use most of it yourself, that's around ₹26,059 saved annually. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, payback lands near 4.5 years — before counting any export income.

DISCOM & net metering in Bihar

Your distribution company is NBPDCL / SBPDCL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹3/kWh — lower than the ₹6.1 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Bihar?

At ₹6.1/kWh, Bihar sits in the middle band — solar pays back solidly, especially if you consume most of your generation during the day. Combined with 5 peak sun hours a day, the numbers work well.
