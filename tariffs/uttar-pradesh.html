What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Uttar Pradesh

A 3 kWp on-grid system in Uttar Pradesh generates roughly 4,527 units per year at local irradiance. At ₹6.5/kWh, if you use most of it yourself, that's around ₹29,426 saved annually. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, payback lands near 4.0 years — before counting any export income.

DISCOM & net metering in Uttar Pradesh

Your distribution company is PVVNL / DVVNL / MVVNL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹2.5/kWh — lower than the ₹6.5 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Uttar Pradesh?

With one of India's higher tariffs at ₹6.5/kWh, Uttar Pradesh offers some of the fastest solar payback in the country. Every unit you generate offsets an expensive grid unit. Combined with 5.3 peak sun hours a day, the numbers work well.
