What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Rajasthan

A 3 kWp on-grid system in Rajasthan generates roughly 5,553 units per year at local irradiance. At ₹6.5/kWh, if you use most of it yourself, that's around ₹36,095 saved annually. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, payback lands near 3.2 years — before counting any export income.

DISCOM & net metering in Rajasthan

Your distribution company is AVVNL / JDVVNL / JVVNL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹2.85/kWh — lower than the ₹6.5 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Rajasthan?

With one of India's higher tariffs at ₹6.5/kWh, Rajasthan offers some of the fastest solar payback in the country. Every unit you generate offsets an expensive grid unit.
