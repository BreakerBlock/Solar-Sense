What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Assam

A 3 kWp on-grid system in Assam generates roughly 4,272 units per year at local irradiance. At ₹5.2/kWh, if you use most of it yourself, that's around ₹22,214 saved annually. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, payback lands near 5.3 years — before counting any export income.

DISCOM & net metering in Assam

Your distribution company is APDCL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹2.8/kWh — lower than the ₹5.2 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Assam?

At ₹5.2/kWh, Assam sits in the middle band — solar pays back solidly, especially if you consume most of your generation during the day. Combined with 5 peak sun hours a day, the numbers work well.
