What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Telangana

A 3 kW on-grid system suits a Telangana home using about 486 units a month. At 5.4 peak sun hours it generates roughly 4,599 units a year. The figures below are the solar calculator's own output for Telangana: it prices the system at its benchmark rate including the net meter, assumes you use 65% of the units yourself at ₹5.3/kWh and export the rest at the ₹2.5/kWh buyback rate. That works out to about ₹19,867 a year and a payback of about 6.6 years after the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy. If your home uses nearly all of the solar units itself, the benefit rises towards ₹24,375 a year and payback falls to about 5.4 years, and a quote below the benchmark shortens it further.

DISCOM & net metering in Telangana

Your distribution company is TSNPDCL / TSSPDCL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹2.5/kWh — lower than the ₹5.3 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Telangana?

At ₹5.3/kWh, Telangana sits in the middle band — solar pays back solidly, especially if you consume most of your generation during the day. Combined with 5.4 peak sun hours a day, the numbers work well. Use the solar subsidy calculator for Telangana to size a system against your own bill.
