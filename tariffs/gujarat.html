What a typical 3 kW system looks like in Gujarat

A 3 kW on-grid system suits a Gujarat home using about 522 units a month. At 5.8 peak sun hours it generates roughly 4,964 units a year. The figures below are the solar calculator's own output for Gujarat: it prices the system at its benchmark rate including the net meter, assumes you use 65% of the units yourself at ₹5.5/kWh and export the rest at the ₹2.25/kWh buyback rate. That works out to about ₹21,657 a year and a payback of about 6.1 years after the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy. If your home uses nearly all of the solar units itself, the benefit rises towards ₹27,302 a year and payback falls to about 4.8 years, and a quote below the benchmark shortens it further.

DISCOM & net metering in Gujarat

Your distribution company is DGVCL / MGVCL / PGVCL. To go solar with net metering, you apply for feasibility approval, install through a registered vendor, and get a bi-directional meter. Surplus units you export earn ₹2.25/kWh — lower than the ₹5.5 you pay, which is why self-consuming your generation is always worth more than exporting it.

Is solar worth it in Gujarat?

At ₹5.5/kWh, Gujarat sits in the middle band — solar pays back solidly, especially if you consume most of your generation during the day. Combined with 5.8 peak sun hours a day, the numbers are strongly in your favour. Use the solar subsidy calculator for Gujarat to size a system against your own bill.
